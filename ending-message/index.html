12. ENDING MESSAGE ▣

Dear all,

In May 2026, FunTech will celebrate its 10th anniversary. When we started, we were almost a student-led company. None of us came from major advertising agencies or fine art universities. Yet we were able to grow, through sheer effort, into the creative company we are proud of today. This was only possible thanks to the trust and support of our clients and partners who chose to work with us. We are deeply grateful. Thank you.

Over the next decade, we will step into a broader field: creative branding, on a larger stage than ever before. As the starting point of that challenge, we renewed our own CI. If this renewal helps you sense our strength in designing craft and language powered by technology, then it is a perfect score for us.

We will continue to fully explore every emerging technology of the moment and relentlessly pursue creativity that sends a jolt of electricity through you. Please look forward to what’s next. And we sincerely appreciate your continued support.
